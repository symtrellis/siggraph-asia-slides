Introduction

We propose a symmetry-enforcement method for voxel-based 3D generation models.

  • Symmetry is enforced during flow generation, not through post-processing.
  • Our method supports arbitrary finite 3D point-group symmetries.
  • We do not need to retrain the flow model or VAE, nor do we use generation-time optimization.
  • We train a lightweight spatial-transform latent mapper to average rotated voxel latents.
  • Our method does not require the preparation of symmetric training data.
