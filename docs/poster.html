SymTRELLIS: Symmetry-Enforced Voxel Latents for 3D Generation

Guangda Ji, Qimin Chen, Qinchan Li, Mingrui Zhao, Kai Wang, Hao Zhang

TL;DR

We propose a symmetry-enforcement method for voxel-based 3D generation models.

  • Symmetry is enforced during flow generation, not through post-processing.
  • Our method supports arbitrary finite 3D point-group symmetries.
  • We do not need to retrain the flow model or VAE, nor do we use generation-time optimization.
  • We train a lightweight spatial-transform latent mapper to average rotated voxel latents.
  • Our method does not require the preparation of symmetric training data.

Velocity Symmetrization

Given a symmetry specification of a rotation axis and a fold, we group-average the velocity of the generation latent space. We do this for both active voxel and shape latent generation stages. The key difficulty is that "rotation" in latent space is not well-defined, as we don't know its transformation law. We solve this by the Spatial-Transform Latent Mapper.

Our mapper approximates the "rotation" operation in the latent space by outputting a set of per-voxel linear coefficients that map the source latent to the target. The mapper is trained on pairs of randomly posed encoded latents. The linear mapping assumption makes it possible to reuse the coefficients at every step of the flow matching process. Our mapper is the key to this rotation operation, as a naïve nearest neighbor copy completely fails.
